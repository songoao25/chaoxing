## Out of scope

- Failures, bans or school policy on the Chaoxing platform itself (ask your school or the platform support).
- Requests to bypass captchas or access controls, or to forge study time.
- Any promise about grades, GPA or passing a course.
- Problems specific to historical versions of the upstream project [Samueli924/chaoxing](https://github.com/Samueli924/chaoxing).
